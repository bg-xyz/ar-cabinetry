Privacy

Last updated October 23, 2026

The short version

The Plywise app does not collect, store or send any personal data. Your room scan stays on your iPhone.

Camera and LiDAR

Plywise uses the camera and LiDAR scanner, through Apple's RoomPlan and ARKit, to measure your walls and show cabinets in your room. That processing happens on the device. The scan, images and measurements are not uploaded anywhere.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracking in the app.

Sending a job to a shop

If a future version lets you send a design to a shop, it will only send what you choose to send, when you tap to send it. This policy will be updated before that feature ships.

TestFlight

During beta testing, Apple's TestFlight may share crash reports and any feedback you submit with the developer, under Apple's privacy policy.

Open source

You can read every line of the app's code on GitHub.
