How it works

From a bare wall to a cut list, in minutes.

01

Choose

Tell it what you're building: a vanity, a built-in bookcase, a media console, a dresser.

02

Scan

Walk the room with a LiDAR iPhone. Walls, doors, windows, ceiling height, the old sink: all measured.

03

See it

Tap a wall. It's planned for the clear space, then locked onto the wall in AR, in real wood.

04

Have it cut

A job package any 3-axis CNC shop can cut: layered DXF sheets, cut list, hardware and a quote.

The collection

Four pieces, one system.

Every piece is a plywood box on the 32 mm system. The planner sizes it to your wall, picks the layout, and keeps the code clearances, so the only choices left are the nice ones: wood, style, grain.

  • Vanity24 to 84 in. Double sink from 60 in. Keeps 15 in from the toilet, wraps the trap with U-drawers.
  • BookcaseFills the wall, bays no wider than 36 in, as tall as the ceiling lets you stand it up.
  • Console36 to 84 in on legs; doors at the ends, drawers between. Slides under a high window.
  • Dresser30 to 72 in, banks of drawers with a shallow one on top.
The craft

Grain that runs on.

Fronts are cut in sequence from one panel and laid out on the sheet exactly as they hang, so the figure flows from drawer to drawer and across the whole face. Open the drawers side by side and the bottoms match too.

  • AcrossHorizontal grain, flowing sideways across every front. The default.
  • WaterfallVertical grain cascading down each stack of drawers.
  • OffEvery part nested on its own for the best yield. The app shows the difference in sheets.
  • FitDados cut a touch loose for glue, and a fit-test coupon in every job, because it's wood.
Four styles

One box, four faces.

Slab

Full-overlay slab fronts with a slim bar pull near the top. The everyday classic.

Frame

The plywood box frames every front; the ply edge is the detail.

Shadow

Fronts set back into the box, a shadow line all round.

Flush

Flush ends, no hardware, push to open. Pure planes.

For makers and CNC shops

Files your router already speaks.

Built for the flat-bed 3-axis routers in thousands of small shops, not a $300k beam saw.

  • LayersOne DXF layer per operation and depth, ready for a VCarve or Aspire toolpath template.
  • MachiningFace-only, no edge boring; operations grouped for machines without a tool changer.
  • HardwareBlum CLIP top hinges and TANDEM runners, drilled on the 32 mm system.
  • JoineryDadoed, captured backs; U-drawers around the trap; fixed shelves in dados; counterbored pulls.
  • SheetsGrain-matched nesting, measured sheet thickness, a fit-test coupon and a quote with yield.

Open source

Free to use, build on and ship.

Plywise is open source under the Apache 2.0 license: the cabinet engine, the iPhone app and the hardware data.

Engine

TypeScript, no dependencies. A short spec becomes every part, hole and dado. 121 tests, including one that checks every joint was actually machined.

iPhone app

Swift, RoomPlan and RealityKit. Pick a build, scan, tap a wall, see it in AR with physically based wood.

Try it

cd engine && npm run samples builds every sample into a full job package with a 3D preview.
