How it works

From a wall to a cut list in minutes

1

Scan

Walk the room with a LiDAR iPhone. Apple's RoomPlan measures every wall, door, window, and the sink you're replacing.

2

Design

Pick a piece and a look. The engine builds every panel, drawer and hinge to fit the wall: no CAD, no guessing.

3

See it

It locks onto your wall in AR with real wood textures. Walk around it, swap walnut for white oak, slide it into place.

4

Send it

Get a job package any 3-axis CNC shop can cut: layered DXF sheets, cut list, hardware list and a quote.

One box, many looks

Every modern cabinet is the same box

Contemporary European cabinetry is a plywood box on the 32 mm system, built a few different ways. Plywise captures the construction choices (how the fronts sit, how you open them, the base, the ends) as switches, so one engine makes every style.

For makers and CNC shops

Files your router already understands

Built for the flat-bed 3-axis routers in thousands of small shops, not a $300k beam saw.

  • Layered DXF per sheet: one layer per operation and depth, ready for a VCarve or Aspire toolpath template
  • Face-only machining (no edge boring), operations grouped for machines without a tool changer
  • Blum CLIP top hinges and TANDEM drawer slides drilled on the 32 mm system
  • U-drawers that wrap the sink trap, dadoed and captured backs, counterbored pulls
  • Grain-aware nesting, true measured sheet thickness, a quote with sheet yield

Open source

Free to use, build on and ship

Plywise is open source under the Apache 2.0 license. The cabinet engine, the iPhone app and the hardware data are all on GitHub.

Engine

TypeScript, no dependencies. A short spec becomes every part, hole and dado. 47 tests, including one that checks every joint was actually machined.

iPhone app

Swift, RoomPlan and RealityKit. Scan, tap a wall, see it in AR with physically based wood textures.

Try it

cd engine && npm run samples builds every sample design into a full job package with a 3D preview.
